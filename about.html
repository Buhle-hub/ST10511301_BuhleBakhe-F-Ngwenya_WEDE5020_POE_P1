About Pretoria Community Kitchen

Pretoria Community Kitchen is a community organisation that aims to help people who experience food insecurity.

The organisation brings volunteers, donors and community members together to prepare and provide meals to people who need support.

Our Vision

A hunger-free Pretoria where every community supports one another.

Our Mission

To provide nutritious meals with love, care & dignity to those in need in Tshwane.

Our Values

Ubuntu, Compassion, Integrity and Community Service.
