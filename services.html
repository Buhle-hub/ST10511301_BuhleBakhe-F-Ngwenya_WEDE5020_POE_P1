Our Services

Pretoria Community Kitchen provides food-related support and opportunities for people to become involved in helping the community.

Community Meals

We prepare and distribute nutritious meals to people and families who need food support.

Food Donations

Individuals and businesses can donate food and kitchen supplies to support our programmes.

Volunteer

Volunteers can assist with food preparation, packing, distribution and community activities.

Sponsorship

Businesses and organisations can support the kitchen through sponsorships and partnerships.

Community Support

We provide information about food assistance and opportunities for community members to help.
